How it works

1. Settings

Your organization's name, address, optional EIN, signer, statement year and thank-you message.

2. Donors & gifts

Add donors with an ID, then log gifts (or paste them in from a bank or giving-platform export): date, donor, cash amount or property description, and whether anything was given in return.

3. Statements

Choose a donor from the dropdown and print, or in Google Sheets use the Statements menu to save one PDF per donor to Drive and optionally email them.

Features

  • Lists each contribution with its date and shows the total, the format IRS Publication 526 describes for an annual acknowledgment

Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, open it with Google Sheets and choose File > Save as Google Sheets. The one-click batch PDF script requires the Google Sheets version.

Does it work in Excel?

Yes: Excel 2010 or newer on Windows or Mac, and LibreOffice. In Excel you choose a donor and print to PDF; the batch script is Google-only.

Is this an official IRS form?

No. There is no official IRS form for donor acknowledgments. The template is built around the information IRS Publication 1771 lists. It's a record-keeping tool, not tax or legal advice.

How many donors and gifts?

Up to 500 donors and 2,000 gift rows. Each statement lists up to 60 gifts, enough for weekly givers.

Is my donor data uploaded anywhere?

No. The file lives in your own Google Drive or computer.
