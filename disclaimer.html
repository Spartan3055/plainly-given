Disclaimer & privacy

Not tax or legal advice

Plainly Given publishes templates and general educational information about donation acknowledgments. Nothing on this site or in our products is tax, legal or accounting advice. We are not attorneys or CPAs, and we are not affiliated with the Internal Revenue Service or any government agency.

Not official IRS forms

The IRS does not issue a form for donor acknowledgments. Our templates are designed around the information listed in IRS Publications 1771 and 526, but they are not IRS forms and do not guarantee that any deduction will be allowed.

Only for gifts actually received

Use these templates only to acknowledge contributions your organization actually received, with accurate dates, amounts and descriptions. Issuing a false or inflated receipt can be illegal.

Your responsibility

Tax rules change, and some gifts (vehicles, high-value or appraised property, gifts to foreign organizations) have additional requirements. Verify current rules at irs.gov and consult a qualified professional.

Privacy

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads, and doesn't collect personal information. Free downloads require no sign-up. Purchases are processed by Gumroad under its own privacy policy. Spreadsheets you buy run in your own Google account or computer, so we never see your donor data.
