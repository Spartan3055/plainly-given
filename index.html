Why this matters in January

For any single gift of $250 or more, a donor can't claim a charitable deduction without a contemporaneous written acknowledgment from your organization, and they need it before they file. That's why most churches and nonprofits send year-end giving statements by January 31. The IRS doesn't provide a form. It lists what the acknowledgment must say. Our templates are built around that list.

What every donation acknowledgment needs

  • The name of your organization
  • The amount of any cash contribution
  • A description (but not the value) of any non-cash contribution
  • A statement that no goods or services were provided, or a description and good-faith estimate of their value, or a statement that only intangible religious benefits were provided
